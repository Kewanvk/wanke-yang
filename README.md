#### Featured Work

**[SMI Market Timing: Symmetric Cost Analysis](https://github.com/Kewanvk/smi-market-timing-analysis)**
20 years of Swiss Market Index daily returns. The standard argument against market timing only shows one side. This analysis adds the other: what happens when you miss the worst days, or both the best and worst. Reproducible Python script, three charts, full methodology.

**[Data Recon: Structured Intelligence from Public Sources](https://github.com/Kewanvk/linkedin-recon-skill)**
Systematic data collection from public professional networks. Raw profiles become multi-dimensional analysis: org structure mapping, relationship network visualization, and actionable insight reports. Built with Python, Claude, and D3.js.

**[Zuoshi Kaopu (做事靠谱)](https://github.com/Kewanvk/zuoshi-kaopu)**
Two actions for research quality control. *Brainstorm*: a second AI model challenges your blind spots, unstated assumptions, and logical gaps. *Verify*: NotebookLM checks your claims against source documents and returns exact quotes with confidence levels. No hallucination, no guessing.
